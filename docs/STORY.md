## Source text (verbatim)

After conquering all six eras and defeating their most powerful bosses, the hero finally reaches the villain's fortress, where his wife is trapped behind a massive crystal barrier. The villain reveals the horrifying truth: the hero has repeated this journey thousands of times, and every victory has reset reality, making the gemstone stronger. The hero was never meant to escape; his endless determination to save his wife was the very thing powering his prison. The final battle begins, but no attack can harm the villain, and his health bar displays IMMORTAL — NO WEAKNESS DETECTED. When the villain mocks him, telling him to try again as he always does, the hero finally understands that he cannot win by following the rules of this world. Instead, he deliberately triggers reset after reset, causing the gemstone to destabilize and reality to glitch. Enemies freeze, levels merge, and the six eras collapse into fragments as the furious villain begs him to fight. The hero replies, "That's the problem. I kept playing your game." With one final reset, the gemstone cracks, its control over the prison breaks, and the entire artificial reality begins to collapse. The hero races through the collapsing world, reaches his wife, and escapes with her through the final opening. He awakens beside her in the real world, where there are no health bars, no enemies, and no signs of the repeating nightmare. When the villain appears and tries to reclaim his power, the hero confronts him and ends his reign of terror permanently. At last, the hero and his wife walk away together into the sunrise, free to live their lives. The credits roll, and the screen fades to black. After a moment, a cursor appears and clicks NEW GAME, but instead of starting another adventure, the screen glitches and displays one final message: ERROR: REALITY INSTANCE NOT FOUND. The ORIGIN logo appears with its gemstone completely dark, leaving players to wonder whether they have truly escaped the loop or merely broken something far more mysterious.

Core premise: a hero is trying to rescue his trapped wife from a reality-bending gemstone's repeating prison.
